Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Balloon Pop Arcade ("the App") handles information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data We Collect

We do not require account sign-up and we do not directly collect personal identity data like your name, email, phone number, or home address in the App.

The App stores gameplay progress locally on your device using local storage (SharedPreferences), including:

  • High score
  • Total balloons popped
  • Games played
  • Stars earned
  • Sound and haptic preference toggles

2. Advertising

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display banner ads and rewarded ads. Advertising partners may collect technical identifiers and usage signals required to serve, limit, and measure ads (for example, device identifiers and ad interaction events), subject to their own policies.

To learn more about how Google processes data, review: Google Privacy Policy.

3. Analytics and Tracking

The current App implementation does not include a dedicated third-party analytics SDK for gameplay analytics. Any measurement related to ads is governed by the advertising provider.

4. Data Retention and Control

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you remove it. You can clear progress from inside the App by using the "Reset all progress" option in Settings.

5. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe information was provided inappropriately, please contact us and we will review the request.

6. Security

We use reasonable measures to reduce risk, but no storage or transmission method is guaranteed to be 100% secure.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use the support contact listed in the App's store listing.
